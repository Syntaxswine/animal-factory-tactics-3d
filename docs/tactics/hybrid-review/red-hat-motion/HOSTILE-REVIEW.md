# Red Hat variants — independent hostile review

Final result: **9/10 for each of the eleven new variants**: horse, goat, bull, cow, donkey, sheep, skunk, pig director, rabbit, dog and hen. The pig foreman's existing faction outfit is unchanged and was not scored as a new variant.

This approval covers the bounded cap and painted-uniform prototype on the existing character meshes and motion study. It is not architect approval, production-animation approval or gameplay integration. The hen remains unarmed; wing weapon contacts, aiming and firing are unsupported. Existing shared-hand quality and compressed kneeling cloth remain limitations.

## Inspection

Independent review used fresh local browser renders alongside refreshed evidence sheets: upright carry, raised-arm aiming/crouching, rear, three-quarter, both actual side profiles, and native gameplay scale. Opposite profiles were checked by reversing the camera around its focus; changing heading alone does not expose the other side because the standard Side camera follows the character. Hen cap placement was also inspected in grey. Frame inspections do not constitute a claim that every recorded video frame was watched.

## Resolved blockers

- Horse: cream cuff and rear neckline remnants, red torso/armband ghosts and a pointed false flank flap were corrected. Faction review status was separated from original-outfit approval.
- Shared mammal variants: inspected cap fits preserve distinctive ears, horns and head silhouettes; uniforms remain coherent through raised-arm poses.
- Director: tailored painting replaced mismatched shared-shirt mapping; nape residue, duplicate rear buckle, a false hanging side patch, enlarged hip bands and an out-of-footprint grey wedge were corrected. Final hip paint is somewhat softer than adjacent folds, but no longer interrupts the garment.
- Hen: tailored chest and wing painting replaced smeared details and flat bands; pale shoulder/skirt remnants were cleaned. The cap was lowered, brought back over the skull and given comb clearance, resolving its perched and excessively forward positions.

The final director opposite-side aiming view and hen grey side cap-clearance view are included as `final-director-opposite-aim.png` and `final-hen-grey-cap-clearance.png`. These are independent browser captures of the accepted revisions.

Mechanical tests, resource lifecycle checks and build results are documented separately by the implementation owner. They support delivery but do not replace the visual judgments above. Architect review remains pending.
