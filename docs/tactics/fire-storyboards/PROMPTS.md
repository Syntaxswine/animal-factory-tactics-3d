5 "CUT OFF": feet and gun still steady. There is a visible small EMPTY AIR GAP after the muzzle, followed by the last detached plume continuing to the RIGHT and rising slightly. No photorealism, toy/plastic rendering, busy landscape, comic speech bubbles, excessive text, extra limbs, mirrored anatomical mistakes, visual UI, serial weapon redesigns or disconnected hands.
