Exact prompt:

Production texture atlas for painterly cargo props in this exact hand-painted character/environment style. Image1 is material style reference; Image2 is approved character brushwork reference only. Create a landscape image with EXACTLY THREE EQUAL COLUMNS and TWO EQUAL ROWS (six square material swatches, NO borders or gaps, dividing lines at x=1/3,2/3 and y=1/2). No characters, props, perspective, text, logos, numbers, decals, labels, outlines, shadows or scene. Every quadrant is a FLAT orthographic material texture filling its cell. TOP LEFT: aged silvery weathered crate WOOD, warm grey-brown broad VERTICAL grain, a few restrained ochre knots; keep broad flat brush planes rather than fine grain. TOP MIDDLE: faded CREAM painted WOOD with some chipped cream paint exposing tawny timber, large soft edged graphic brush patches, vertical grain; restrained wear on 15 percent of area. TOP RIGHT: dark desaturated OLIVE painted WOOD, broad uneven brushed patches and worn tawny grain, not camouflage. BOTTOM LEFT: OXIDE RED painted STEEL, rusty warm red/plum, a few dark exposed iron chips, matte broad painterly strokes. BOTTOM MIDDLE: muted warm OCHRE YELLOW painted STEEL, broad golden/sienna brush planes, sparse grey iron scuffs, no stripes. BOTTOM RIGHT: worn warm CREAM painted STEEL, broad ivory/taupe paint planes, localized dark-grey chips and restrained rust; no fine crackle. Shared confident angular hand-painted finish like approved horse shirt and overalls. Quiet material midtones with selective stronger wear, NOT shiny metal, not noisy procedural texture, not photorealism, not tiny dense scratches. Each cell genuinely has a different material/wear treatment, not a hue shifted copy. Flat texture atlas only.
